Witlings Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

This policy covers Witlings, an iPhone app published by Trusty Genius LLC.

The short version: Witlings does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics.

What stays on your phone

Your scores, streak and history are saved on your device only. They are not sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

If you subscribe, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple through your Apple Account. We never see your name, email address or payment details. Apple tells the app only whether a subscription is active. Apple's handling of your information is described in Apple's privacy policy.

Sharing

If you choose to share a result card, the app hands a picture to the iPhone's share sheet and you decide where it goes. The app does not post anything on your behalf.

Children

Witlings does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.

Contact

For questions about this policy, use the App Support link on the Witlings page in the App Store.
